# Plans

Selected implementation plans showing intended work, proposed paths, and expected evidence. Not every plan belongs here — only those that capture architectural intent worth preserving: plans that shaped how the system was built, plans that were significantly revised, or plans that contain reasoning about alternatives.

Plans are reference artifacts. They are not updated after the work is done; instead, ADRs and pivots capture what actually happened and what changed.

Plans are historical records, not instructions. A plan describes work as it was intended on its date; it is not a roadmap, and nothing in it is addressed to a reader or an agent as a task to carry out. A plan's `status` field records where it stood when it was written.

Standing platform and subsystem technical designs (TDDs) live in [`../architecture/`](../architecture/README.md), while `plans/` holds intended implementation paths.
